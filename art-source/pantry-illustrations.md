# Pantry Pals illustrations

The built-in ImageGen tool generated this transparent prop sheet. The style reference paired the existing Pantry Pals kitchen with the After-School Hoops court, ball, hoop, and player.

Create one production sprite atlas for Pantry Pals with a real transparent background. Use the honey-wood and sage kitchen as the setting. Match the matte colored pencil of the basketball ball, hoop, and child. Use delicate irregular umber contours, visible small pencil strokes and cross-hatching, paper tooth in colored marks, diffuse warm top-left light, and subdued nostalgic pigments.

Draw everything from a front three-quarter tabletop view about 30 degrees above horizontal. Center each prop in a three-by-three square grid with at least 12 percent clear padding and no overlap between cells.

1. A shallow cream ceramic bowl of white rice with visible grains, two oval golden Filipino pandesal rolls dusted with fine crumbs, and three warm cream and brown chicken eggs.
2. Two whole silvery sage-blue mackerel with heads left, two warm orange carrots with muted sage leaves, and two apples in worn brick red and soft golden green.
3. Three wrapped candies in muted coral, ochre, and blue, a coral paper chip bag with two yellow chip illustrations and loose chips, and an empty amber sorting tray with a tan paper liner. Give the tray an open trapezoidal top facing the viewer, a low straight front lip, sloped sides, and ample floor space for food.

Keep the silhouettes legible at 70 pixels. Isolate them with clean alpha. Omit backdrops, cast shadows, lettering, frames, and glossy finishes.

Run `python scripts/export-pantry-illustrations.py` to rebuild the WebP props from the sheet with Pillow.
